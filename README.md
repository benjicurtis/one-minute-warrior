One Minute Warrior

Embark on a fast-paced, turn-based text adventure RPG designed for one or two players. 

In One Minute Warrior, you'll journey through three treacherous biomes, each guarded by a fearsome boss. Test your strategy and reflexes as you face off in thrilling battles. 

Team up in two-player mode to combine your strength and take down the toughest enemies together. Are you ready to become a legend in just one minute?
